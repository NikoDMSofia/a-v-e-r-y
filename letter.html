The letter.

Not to convince you. Not to make you feel guilty. Just to finally say what stayed inside me.

A small note

Read this at your own pace. There is no answer expected at the end.

August 20, 2026

Avery,

I didn’t make this page to convince you of anything. I made it because sometimes the words we don’t manage to say in time are the ones that stay with us the longest.

Two months may not seem like a long time. But sometimes a person doesn’t need years to become important to you.

You became important to me.

Not because of one big moment, but because of all the little things between us. Our conversations. Our laughter. The looks we shared. The touches. The moments when we were simply together, and that was enough.

There are things I probably should have told you more often.

I should have hugged you more often for no reason.
Told you that I missed you.
Shown you how much you meant to me instead of assuming you simply knew.

Maybe that’s where I went wrong.
I knew what I felt for you, but I didn’t understand soon enough that you didn’t just need to know it — you needed to feel it.

When I look at these photos now, I don’t just see memories. I see two people who shared beautiful moments. I see you as I came to know you. And I see a part of my life that I don’t want to simply erase.

I don’t know what will happen from here.

But I know what our time together meant to me.

And that is why this page exists.
Not as a goodbye.
Not as a way to make you feel guilty.
But simply as a place where I could leave some of what I didn’t manage to show you well enough while you were beside me.

You matter to me, Avery.
Much more than I managed to show you.

Niki
